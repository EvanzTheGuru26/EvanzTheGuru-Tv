Register Your Business in Kenya

A practical guide to business name and company registration through the official Business Registration Service and eCitizen platform.

📅 Last reviewed: October 2026

Registration procedures, requirements and government fees can change. Always confirm the latest information displayed by the official Business Registration Service before submitting an application or making payment.

🏢 What Is BRS?

The Business Registration Service (BRS) administers Kenya's Companies Registry and related business registration services.

Business registration services are available online through the BRS eCitizen platform.

📊 What Can You Register?

👤 Business Name

The simplest form under which an individual can operate a business. A business name is not a separate legal entity.

🏢 Private Limited Company

A separate legal entity commonly used by businesses operating with shareholders and directors.

🤝 LLP

A Limited Liability Partnership combines partnership arrangements with limited liability features.

🌍 Foreign Company

Foreign companies can register to operate in Kenya subject to the applicable requirements.

💰 Current Registration Fees

The current BRS Companies Registry fee schedule lists:

Business Name Registration KSh 950
Private Limited Company KSh 10,650
Limited Liability Partnership KSh 25,000
Official Search — Company KSh 650
Official Search — Business Name KSh 250

⚠️ Fees can change. Confirm the current amount displayed on the official BRS platform before payment.

👤 How to Register a Business Name

STEP 1 — Open BRS eCitizen

Visit the official Business Registration Service platform and sign in using your eCitizen account.

STEP 2 — Select Companies Registry

Open Companies Registry Services and select the option to make an application.

STEP 3 — Choose Business Name Registration

Select registration of a business name and start the application.

STEP 4 — Submit Your Preferred Names

Enter between three and five preferred business names in order of priority, as requested by the BRS system.

STEP 5 — Complete the Application

Provide the required proprietor and business information. Review everything carefully before submission.

STEP 6 — Sign the Generated Form

Where required, download the system-generated registration form, sign it, scan it and upload it back to the platform.

STEP 7 — Pay the Government Fee

Complete payment using the official payment options provided by the BRS platform.

STEP 8 — Download Your Certificate

Once the application has been successfully processed, access the available output through the official platform.

🏢 How to Register a Private Limited Company

STEP 1 — Access BRS

Log into the official BRS eCitizen platform.

STEP 2 — Select Private Limited Company

Choose the private limited company registration service.

STEP 3 — Provide Preferred Names

Submit three to five preferred company names in the order of priority requested by the system.

STEP 4 — Enter Company Details

Provide the required company, director, shareholder, shareholding and registered-office information.

STEP 5 — Upload Required Documents

Complete and upload the forms and supporting documents requested by the BRS system.

STEP 6 — Verify Your Information

Carefully check all information before submitting the application.

STEP 7 — Make Payment

Pay the applicable government fee through the official BRS payment process.

STEP 8 — Download the Output

Once processing is complete, access the certificate or other generated documents through the official platform.

💡 Choosing a Business Name

BRS has rules governing acceptable business and company names.


  • Avoid names that are identical to an existing registered entity.
  • Avoid names that are too similar to existing registered entities or trademarks.
  • Offensive or abusive names are not accepted.
  • Certain words, including terms associated with government, cooperatives and trade unions, have restrictions.
  • A private limited company's name must end with Limited or Ltd.
  • Consider checking trademarks before settling on a business or company name.

🔎 Official Business Search

BRS also provides official search services for registered companies and business names.

This can be useful when checking information about an existing registered business or company.

📌 Important After Registration

Registering a business or company is only one part of becoming fully operational.


  • Check whether you need a county business permit.
  • Check applicable KRA tax obligations.
  • Check sector-specific licences or permits where applicable.
  • Keep your registration documents safely.
  • Keep company and business information updated when changes occur.

🔐 Protect Your eCitizen Account

Never give your eCitizen password, PIN, OTP or account recovery information to another person.


ETG TV can guide you through the process, but you should remain in control of your own government account and security credentials.
