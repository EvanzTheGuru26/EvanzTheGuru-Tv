How to Apply for a Kenyan Passport

A simple step-by-step guide to applying for, renewing or replacing a Kenyan passport through the official Directorate of Immigration Services platform.

📅 Last reviewed: October 2026

Passport requirements, fees, appointment procedures and government systems can change. Always confirm the latest information on the official Directorate of Immigration Services and eCitizen platforms before making payment or attending an appointment.

🛂 Before You Start

Kenyan passport applications are submitted online through the Directorate of Immigration Services on eCitizen.

After completing the application and payment, applicants are required to book an appointment and attend a designated passport processing centre for document submission and biometric enrolment.

📋 Common Requirements

Requirements can vary depending on whether you are applying for a new passport, renewal, replacement, a lost passport or a mutilated passport.


  • Duly completed passport application Form 19.
  • Two payment invoices.
  • Original birth certificate and a copy for Kenyan citizens by birth.
  • Original National ID and a copy for adults.
  • Parents' documents where required.
  • Consent form and supporting documents for minors where applicable.
  • Previous passport and a copy for replacement applications.
  • Additional documents may be required depending on the application category.

💰 Current Passport Fees

The Directorate of Immigration Services currently lists the following ordinary passport fees:

Passport Fee
Ordinary – 34 pages KSh 7,550
Ordinary – 50 pages KSh 9,550
Ordinary – 66 pages KSh 12,050
Diplomatic passport KSh 15,050
Lost or mutilated passport KSh 20,050

⚠️ Fees may change. Confirm the amount displayed on the official application before payment.

🚀 Step-by-Step Application

STEP 1 — Log in to eCitizen

Visit the official eCitizen platform and log into your account. If you do not have an account, create one using the official registration process.

STEP 2 — Open Directorate of Immigration Services

Access the Directorate of Immigration Services section and select the passport application service.

STEP 3 — Select the Appropriate Application

Select the service that matches your situation, such as a new passport, renewal or replacement.

STEP 4 — Complete the Application

Enter your information carefully and upload or provide the documents requested by the system.

Important: Check your names, dates, identification details and other information carefully before submitting.

STEP 5 — Pay the Required Fee

Use the payment options provided through the official platform and confirm the amount before completing payment.

STEP 6 — Download and Print Your Documents

After completing the application, download and print the required application forms and payment invoices.

STEP 7 — Book Your Appointment

Book an appointment through the official system and select an available passport processing centre.

Processing centres include Nairobi (Nyayo House), Kisumu, Mombasa, Eldoret, Nakuru, Embu, Kisii, Kericho and Bungoma.

STEP 8 — Attend Your Appointment

Attend the selected centre in person with your required original documents and copies.

Your photograph and biometric information will be captured as part of the enrolment process.

STEP 9 — Wait for Processing

Immigration will process your application. Processing times can vary, so monitor your official application status and notifications.

STEP 10 — Collect Your Passport

Follow the official notification and collection instructions provided by the Directorate of Immigration Services.

📌 Important Tips

  • Make sure the information on your application matches your official documents.
  • Keep your payment receipts and application documents safe.
  • Attend your appointment with the required original documents.
  • Do not pay unofficial agents or individuals promising to "guarantee" a passport.
  • Confirm the latest requirements and fees before making payment.
