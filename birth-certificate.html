Birth Certificate & Civil Registration

A practical guide to birth registration and birth certificate services in Kenya through the official Civil Registration Services and eCitizen platforms.

📅 Last reviewed: October 2026

Government procedures, requirements and fees can change. Always confirm the latest information on the official Civil Registration Services platform before submitting an application or making payment.

📜 What Is Civil Registration?

Civil Registration Services (CRS) is responsible for the registration of births and deaths in Kenya and the issuance of birth and death certificates.

Birth registration establishes a person's legal identity and provides an important foundation for accessing government and other essential services.

🗂️ Common Birth Services

👶 Current Birth Certificate

For births registered within the applicable registration period, including applications for a birth certificate.

⏰ Late Birth Registration

For births that were not registered within six months from the date of birth.

✏️ Change of Particulars

For eligible corrections or changes to information in birth records.

🌍 Birth Abroad

Registration of births of Kenyan citizens that occurred outside Kenya.

📋 Current Birth Certificate Requirements

For a current birth certificate application, the official Civil Registration Services information lists the following important information/documents:


  • Knowledge of the child's date of birth.
  • Name of the mother.
  • Place of birth.
  • Acknowledgement of the Birth Notification (ABN).
  • Tangible evidence of the ABN number where the notification number is missing.

Additional documents or information may be requested by the registrar depending on the circumstances of the application.

💰 Current Fees

The official Civil Registration Services information currently lists:

Birth certificate KSh 200
Registration of name / alteration of particulars KSh 1,000
Late birth registration KSh 500 + KSh 200 certificate

⚠️ Fees depend on the specific service. Confirm the amount displayed on the official government platform before payment.

🚀 How to Apply for a Birth Certificate

STEP 1 — Open eCitizen

Visit the official eCitizen platform and sign in to your account.

STEP 2 — Open Civil Registration Services

Select Civil Registration Services and choose the appropriate birth service.

STEP 3 — Select the Correct Application

Choose the service that matches your situation, such as a current birth certificate or another applicable birth registration service.

STEP 4 — Enter the Required Information

Enter the child's and parent's information carefully. Make sure the information agrees with the available official records and birth notification.

STEP 5 — Upload or Provide Required Documents

Follow the instructions displayed by the system and provide the required supporting information or documents.

STEP 6 — Make the Required Payment

Confirm the applicable government fee and complete payment through the official payment options provided.

STEP 7 — Print the Application Documents

Where instructed, print the application form and payment invoice and follow the submission instructions.

STEP 8 — Submit for Processing

Submit the required documents to the appropriate registrar or follow the collection/submission instructions provided by the official system.

STEP 9 — Receive Your Certificate

Follow the official instructions for collection or accessing the completed service.

⏰ If the Birth Was Not Registered Within 6 Months

A birth that was not registered within six months is treated as a late birth registration.


The official requirements can include supporting evidence such as:


  • Clinic or antenatal register information.
  • Religious certificate where applicable.
  • Parents' identity cards.
  • Evidence concerning the parent's marital status where required.
  • School records or identity documents for adult applicants, where applicable.
  • Any other document requested by the registrar.

Late registration has a different procedure from a normal current-birth application.

🌍 Birth of a Kenyan Citizen Abroad

Kenyan citizens whose children were born outside Kenya may have a separate birth-registration process.


Requirements can include the foreign birth certificate, the child's passport, identification documents of the Kenyan parent, and the prescribed application form.

The requirements and fees differ from ordinary domestic birth registration, so use the official Civil Registration Services guidance for this category.

📌 Important Tips

  • Check the spelling of names carefully before submitting.
  • Make sure dates and places of birth are entered correctly.
  • Keep your payment receipt and application reference.
  • Keep copies of important supporting documents.
  • Do not pay unofficial agents promising to "speed up" a government application.
  • Always confirm current requirements and fees on the official government platform.
