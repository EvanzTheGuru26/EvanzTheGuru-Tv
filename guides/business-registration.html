Business Registration Guide in Kenya

Learn how to start a business registration application online, understand the common business types, and prepare the information you may need.

Open Official BRS Portal Back to ETG TV Home

1. Use the official portal

Kenya's Business Registration Service (BRS) provides business registration services through the eCitizen system.

Official website: brs.ecitizen.go.ke

Use the official portal to check the current service, application requirements, fees and payment instructions. Fees and procedures may change.

2. Understand the common business types

Business name / sole proprietorship

Often suitable for an individual operating a small business under a registered business name. A business name is not a separate limited-liability company.

Partnership

May suit two or more people carrying on a business together. Understand the partners' legal responsibilities before applying.

Private limited company

May suit a business that needs a company structure. It has additional registration and ongoing compliance obligations.

Choose the structure that matches your circumstances. If liability, ownership or tax treatment is unclear, seek advice from a qualified professional before registering.

3. Prepare before applying

  • An active eCitizen account and access to the official BRS service.
  • Your preferred business or company name, where name selection is required.
  • Accurate identification and contact details for the applicant and relevant owners or directors.
  • The proposed business activity and address information requested by the portal.
  • Any additional details or documents required for the business type you select.
  • A payment method accepted by the official portal.

Requirements differ by registration type. Follow the checklist displayed for your specific application.

4. General application steps

  1. Open the official BRS eCitizen portal.
  2. Sign in to your eCitizen account or follow the official account-creation process.
  3. Open the relevant business registration service.
  4. Select the appropriate registration type and provide the requested details.
  5. Review names, identification details, ownership information and contact details carefully.
  6. Submit the application and pay using the instructions shown on the official portal.
  7. Save the application reference and payment confirmation.
  8. Check the application status and download the official registration documents when they become available.
Submitting an application does not automatically mean it has been approved. Follow any instructions from BRS if additional information is requested.

5. After registration

  • Keep your official registration documents and payment receipts safe.
  • Check whether your business needs a county business permit or other sector-specific licences.
  • Check applicable KRA tax registration and filing obligations.
  • Maintain accurate business records and meet any ongoing filing requirements that apply to your business structure.
  • Use official government channels to confirm current obligations.

6. Common mistakes to avoid

  • Entering names or identification details incorrectly.
  • Selecting a business structure without understanding its responsibilities.
  • Paying through unofficial agents or personal numbers not specified by the official portal.
  • Assuming registration automatically provides every permit or licence needed to operate.
  • Sharing your eCitizen password, one-time PIN or payment PIN with another person.
