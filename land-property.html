Land & Property Services in Kenya

A practical guide to land searches, transfers, title services, registration and other land-related services available through Kenya's digital land-service platforms.

Last reviewed: October 2026

🏠 What is Ardhisasa?

Ardhisasa is the Government of Kenya's online land information and transaction platform. It allows citizens, stakeholders and other users to interact with land information and access various services provided by the State Department for Lands and related institutions.

Available services include land registration, land administration, physical planning, survey and mapping, valuation, adjudication and National Land Commission services.

🔎 Services You Can Access

🔍 Land Search

Conduct an official search to verify ownership details and identify registered interests such as charges, cautions or restrictions.

🔄 Land Transfer

Registration of a change in property ownership arising from transactions such as sale, gift or inheritance.

📜 Title Services

Services relating to registration, replacement of lost titles and other title-related land records.

🏦 Charges & Discharges

Registration or removal of financial interests such as mortgages or loans against property.

🗺️ Survey & Mapping

Survey-related services including plans, mutations, subdivisions and other mapping processes.

🏛️ Land Administration

Services including land rent, leases, consents, cautions and other land administration matters.

🔎 How to Conduct an Official Land Search

An official land search is one of the most important due-diligence steps before purchasing or otherwise transacting on property. The State Department for Lands says the search helps verify ownership and identify registered encumbrances.

  1. Open the official Ardhisasa website.
  2. Register or log in to your Ardhisasa account.
  3. Navigate to the relevant land search service.
  4. Enter the required property or parcel details.
  5. Provide the required identification information and documents.
  6. Submit the application and follow the payment instructions provided by the official platform.
  7. Check your application status and obtain the search result when it becomes available.
Important: A land search should be treated as a key part of due diligence. Do not rely only on a seller's copy of a title deed or verbal assurances about ownership.

🔄 Land Transfer

A land transfer officially records a change of ownership resulting from a sale, gift, inheritance or another lawful transaction.

Common requirements

  • Executed transfer instrument
  • Original title where applicable
  • Identification documents
  • KRA PIN certificate
  • Valuation documentation where required
  • Land rent clearance where applicable
  • Consent where required
  • Stamp duty payment documentation
  • Passport-size photographs where required

General process

  1. Carry out due diligence and obtain an official land search.
  2. Confirm the property's ownership and registered interests.
  3. Obtain any required consent or supporting documentation.
  4. Prepare and execute the transfer documents.
  5. Submit the transaction through the applicable official land-service process.
  6. Complete valuation, stamp duty assessment and payment where applicable.
  7. Complete registration and obtain the relevant updated ownership documentation.
Fee warning: Land transaction costs can depend on the transaction and property value. Always confirm the current official charges before making payment.

🏦 Charges & Discharges

A charge records a lender's legal interest in property used as security for a loan. A discharge removes that registered financial claim after the relevant obligation has been settled.

Charge

Typical supporting documents can include the charge instrument, consent, spousal consent where applicable, original title, proof of stamp duty, identification and KRA PIN documentation.

Discharge

A discharge normally requires the discharge instrument together with the relevant title, identification, PIN and payment documentation.

Because the exact requirements depend on the transaction, confirm the current requirements with the official land registry or Ardhisasa before proceeding.

🗺️ Subdivision & Survey Services

Land subdivision, amalgamation, mutations and other survey-related processes can involve licensed professionals and government approvals.

  • Use a properly licensed surveyor where professional survey work is required.
  • Confirm ownership before beginning a subdivision process.
  • Obtain the required consent or approvals.
  • Ensure mutation or survey documents are properly prepared.
  • Submit documents through the applicable official process.

Some survey services on Ardhisasa are specifically intended for registered professionals such as licensed surveyors.

🏛️ Land Control Board Consent

Certain transactions involving agricultural land may require Land Control Board consent. The State Department for Lands identifies transactions such as sales, leases, transfers and subdivisions among those for which consent may be required.

Do not assume consent is unnecessary. Before completing an agricultural land transaction, confirm whether Land Control Board consent applies to your particular transaction.

💰 About Land-Service Fees

Government charges vary according to the service and transaction. For example, the State Department for Lands currently lists an official search certificate at KSh 1,000.

Land transfers may involve registration fees, title-related fees and stamp duty, with the exact amount depending on the transaction and applicable rules.

Always confirm the amount shown on the official government platform before paying.

⚠️ Land Buying Safety Checklist

  • Conduct an official land search before paying for property.
  • Confirm that the seller is the registered proprietor or is legally authorised to transact.
  • Check for cautions, restrictions, charges and other encumbrances.
  • Verify the parcel details and boundaries.
  • Use qualified professionals where legal or survey expertise is required.
  • Do not make large payments based only on WhatsApp conversations or informal agreements.
  • Keep copies of agreements, receipts, searches and official correspondence.
  • Use official government platforms for government payments.
