How to Apply for a Certificate of Good Conduct

A practical guide to applying for a Police Clearance Certificate through the official DCI eCitizen service.

🛡️ What is a Certificate of Good Conduct?

A Certificate of Good Conduct, also known as a Police Clearance Certificate, is an official document issued through the Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI). It is commonly requested for employment, immigration, professional and other official purposes.

📋 What You Need

For a Kenyan adult applicant, you should generally have:


  • An active eCitizen account
  • Your original National ID
  • Access to the required payment method
  • The ability to print the application invoice and C24 form
  • Access to the selected fingerprinting location

Requirements can differ for minors, foreign residents, refugees and applicants outside Kenya. Always check the latest DCI instructions for your category.

1️⃣ Access the Official DCI eCitizen Service

Go to the official DCI eCitizen service and sign in using your eCitizen account.


Open DCI eCitizen

2️⃣ Start Your Application

Follow the instructions provided on the official portal and select the appropriate Police Clearance Certificate application. Kenyan adults should use the adult application option.


Carefully enter your personal information and review it before submitting.

3️⃣ Select Your Fingerprinting Location

During the application, select the available location where you will have your fingerprints taken.


DCI currently lists Huduma Centres countrywide and DCI Headquarters among the locations available for fingerprint taking, with other locations indicated through the application system.

4️⃣ Make the Required Payment

Select the available payment method shown on the official application and complete the required payment.


Always confirm the amount displayed on the official government portal before making payment.

5️⃣ Download and Print Your Documents

After completing the application and payment, download and print:


  • Two copies of your invoice
  • One copy of the C24 form printed on both sides on A4 paper

6️⃣ Go for Fingerprinting

Present the required documents together with your original National ID at the fingerprinting location selected during your application.


Your fingerprints are taken for processing of the Police Clearance Certificate.

7️⃣ Wait for the Certificate

After fingerprinting and processing, monitor your eCitizen account and phone for notification.


DCI states that applicants receive an SMS notification when the certificate is ready for download.

8️⃣ Download Your Certificate

Once notified that your Police Clearance Certificate is ready, log in to your eCitizen account and follow the instructions provided to access your certificate.

⚠️ Important Things to Remember

  • Check your personal details carefully before submitting.
  • Keep your eCitizen login credentials private.
  • Never share your password or OTP with anyone.
  • Confirm payment information on the official portal.
  • Follow the latest DCI instructions because procedures and requirements may change.
