NTSA Services Guide

A beginner-friendly guide to accessing transport-related services in Kenya, understanding common requirements and navigating official online services safely.

Important information

Service names, eligibility rules, fees, required documents and procedures can change. Confirm the current requirements on the official NTSA website before applying or paying. This guide is for general information and is not an official NTSA publication.

🚗 What is NTSA?

The National Transport and Safety Authority (NTSA) is Kenya's government authority responsible for aspects of road transport administration and road safety. Its services cover drivers, motor vehicles and other transport-related matters.

Use the official website to find current services and instructions.

💻 How to Get Started Online

STEP 1

Open the official website

Type ntsa.go.ke into your browser. Check the address carefully before entering personal information.

STEP 2

Choose the relevant service

Find the service you need and read its current eligibility rules, requirements and instructions.

STEP 3

Sign in through the authorised platform

If directed to eCitizen or another authorised government service, use the official link and your own account. Follow any identity verification instructions shown.

STEP 4

Prepare the required information

Depending on the service, you may need identification details, vehicle registration information, licence details or supporting documents. Do not assume every service has the same requirements.

STEP 5

Review before submitting

Check that your information is accurate. Read the displayed charges and payment instructions before confirming an application.

STEP 6

Save your confirmation

Keep the application reference, receipt or confirmation provided by the official platform. Follow the instructions for checking progress or attending an appointment, if required.

🪪 Driving Licence Guidance

Requirements depend on the type of licence and the service being requested. Before starting, confirm the current process on the official NTSA website.

  • Check eligibility and the applicable licence category.
  • Confirm whether training, testing, medical information or other supporting steps apply.
  • Check the current fee and the official payment method.
  • Follow the official instructions for appointments, tests or collection where applicable.
  • Keep your application reference and payment receipt.

Do not rely on unofficial promises of instant licences or guaranteed approval.

🚘 Vehicle Registration and Ownership Transfer

When registering a vehicle or transferring ownership, first identify the exact service required and confirm the current process with NTSA.

  • Check that the vehicle registration details are accurate.
  • Confirm which identification and supporting documents are required from the parties involved.
  • Follow the official process for the seller, buyer or authorised representative, as applicable.
  • Review any displayed fees before paying.
  • Keep the confirmation and check the status using the official platform.
Before buying a vehicle

Verify the vehicle and its ownership information through authorised channels. Do not rely solely on screenshots, copies of documents or claims made by a seller.

🔍 Vehicle Inspection

Inspection requirements depend on the vehicle and the service involved. Consult NTSA for current eligibility, booking arrangements, applicable charges and the documents you should bring.

  1. Confirm whether inspection is required for your situation.
  2. Follow the official booking or appointment instructions, if applicable.
  3. Check the vehicle and documents before attending.
  4. Keep any inspection confirmation or report issued to you.

🔐 Protect Your Account and Money

  • Use official government websites and authorised payment instructions.
  • Never share your password, M-Pesa PIN or one-time verification code with an agent.
  • Do not pay a person who promises guaranteed approval or asks you to bypass official procedures.
  • Check the recipient and amount before confirming a payment.
  • Use your own account and provide truthful information.
  • Sign out when using a shared computer and avoid saving your password on it.
  • If a transaction fails, check the official account or support channel before paying again.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is this the official NTSA website?

No. ETG TV provides general guidance. Use the official NTSA website for applications, requirements and authoritative information.

Are NTSA services and fees the same for everyone?

Not necessarily. Requirements and charges depend on the particular service and your circumstances. Check the current information shown by the official platform.

What if I cannot access my account?

Use the official account recovery or support instructions. Do not give your password or verification codes to someone claiming they can recover your account.

What if my payment or application is pending?

Keep your reference and receipt, check the application status through the official platform and contact authorised support if necessary. Avoid making a duplicate payment until you have checked the transaction status.

Can ETG TV guarantee that my application will be approved?

No. Only the relevant authority can determine the outcome of an application. Accurate information and following the official procedure do not guarantee approval.
